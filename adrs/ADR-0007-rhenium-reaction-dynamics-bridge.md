# ADR-0007: Rhenium Catalyst as a Reaction Dynamics Bridge

*Status: Accepted; route and bridge-count validation outstanding*
*Decision date: 2026-11-06*
*Last updated: 2026-11-12*

Related documents: [Intermediate Products](../intermediate-products.md), [Chemical Products and Processes](../chemical-processes.md), [Research and Progression](../research-progression.md).

## Context

The intermediate-products source marks “Decision A1” committed for a high-performance rhenium-catalyst powder used to enable the Reaction Dynamics Facility. The design rationale uses rhenium's high melting point and high-pressure catalysis as the material's capability hook: understanding how reactions move, not only whether they occur. The exact facility set and bridge-product count are inconsistent elsewhere in the source.

## Decision

Include high-performance rhenium-catalyst powder as a committed Reaction Dynamics bridge selection. Treat its detailed production route and the number of required bridge products as unresolved until the full domain roster is reconciled.

## Options Considered

The source records the selected rhenium product and capability rationale but does not preserve a complete alternatives analysis. Do not infer that alternative catalysts or bridge materials were formally rejected.

## Rationale

The product represents a distinct capability and links molybdenite/rhenium recovery to reaction engineering and research infrastructure.

## Consequences

- Positive: Reaction Dynamics has a concrete industrial prerequisite.
- Trade-off: the proposed molybdenite-residue extraction and catalyst-bed pathway adds a specialized chain whose end-to-end need must be validated.
- Follow-up: resolve the bridge roster's five-versus-six count, map every bridge to its domain facility, and validate the route and educational claims.
