# Factorio Overhaul Concept V2
## Industrial Ecosystems and Process Engineering

---

# Executive Summary

This project is not intended to be:

- Factorio but larger
- Pyanodon's but harder
- GTNH but in Factorio
- Angel's with more ores

The goal is to create a coherent industrial ecosystem where every major mechanic supports a common vision.

The central design philosophy is:

> Progression should come from understanding and choosing industrial strategies rather than unlocking longer recipe chains.

The player should feel less like a factory builder and more like an industrial engineer.

---

# Grand Design Philosophy

One of the greatest strengths of large overhaul mods such as GTNH is the enormous breadth of content and progression.

One of the greatest weaknesses is that they can feel stitched together from many independent systems.

This project aims to avoid that.

Every major system should reinforce every other system:

- resource extraction
- refining
- metallurgy
- chemistry
- power generation
- pollution
- military pressure
- semiconductors
- endgame research

Nothing should exist merely because it is realistic or because another mod did it.

Every system should support the same industrial philosophy.

---

# Core Design Insight

Most overhauls create complexity through:

- additional intermediates
- additional resources
- additional processing tiers
- additional recipes

This mod should create complexity through:

- decisions
- tradeoffs
- process selection
- industrial optimisation

The guiding rule:

> If all experienced players choose the same solution, the problem has been solved.

> If experienced players continuously debate the best solution, the system is healthy.

---

# Design Pillars

## Pillar 1: Multiple Industrial Processes

Every major product should have multiple viable production methods.

Not upgrades.

Not tiers.

Alternatives.

---

## Pillar 2: Tradeoffs Instead of Progression

Every industrial process should optimise different constraints:

- throughput
- pollution
- power
- space
- infrastructure cost
- resource efficiency
- military pressure

No option should dominate all others.

---

## Pillar 3: Processes Instead of Recipes

The player should ask:

> Which process should I use?

instead of:

> Which recipe do I unlock next?

---

## Pillar 4: Refining Creates Options

Better refining should create additional options.

Not simply higher yields.

The player should continuously evaluate:

- ore availability
- power availability
- logistics complexity
- military pressure
- desired outputs

---

## Pillar 5: Pollution as an Optimisation Axis

Pollution should not merely be punishment.

Pollution should be a strategic choice.

Potential examples:

Dirty Process:
- higher throughput
- lower capital cost
- higher pollution

Clean Process:
- lower pollution
- more infrastructure
- lower throughput

Both must remain viable.

---

## Pillar 6: Military Emerges From Industry

Military challenge should emerge from industrial decisions.

Not simply from elapsed time.

The factory's industrial profile should partially determine the defensive profile required.

---

## Pillar 7: Equilibrium Over Disposal

This is one of the defining pillars of the project.

Industrial systems should be capable of reaching a balanced steady state.

Storage may exist.

Voiding may exist.

Neither should be required.

The optimal solution should be:

> equilibrium

rather than:

> disposal

# Inspirations

## Satisfactory

The most important lesson from Satisfactory is not factory building.

It is alternate recipes.

The best alternate recipes are not upgrades.

They are tradeoffs.

The correct solution depends on:

- available resources
- power
- logistics
- space
- existing factory design

This philosophy should influence every production chain.

---

## Angel's Refining

Angel's contains one of the strongest industrial concepts ever implemented in a Factorio mod.

Many players interpreted the system as:

> byproduct management

The deeper reality was:

> refinery equilibrium

The beauty of the design was that a complete refinery could gradually balance itself and avoid waste entirely.

This project should heavily embrace this philosophy.

### Discoverability Problem

The primary issue with Angel's was not the design itself.

The issue was that many players never discovered the grand design behind the system.

This mod should actively teach players:

- how equilibrium systems work
- how refining chains interact
- why byproducts exist
- how balanced industrial ecosystems emerge

Complexity is not the enemy.

Hidden complexity is.

---

## GTNH

GTNH demonstrates:

- deep industrial progression
- meaningful milestones
- educational value
- real-world materials

However, GTNH also demonstrates how complexity can become overwhelming when systems are not obviously connected.

This project should seek the same depth while maintaining a stronger overall vision.

One major lesson:

> The player should unlock capabilities, not recipes.

Examples:

- first refinery
- first chemical plant
- first cleanroom
- first semiconductor fab

These become memorable milestones.

---

## Dyson Sphere Program

DSP provides perhaps the strongest inspiration for the endgame.

The player's industrial growth serves a larger purpose.

The factory exists to create computational power.

The player is not simply building machines.

The player is contributing to a grand project.

This feeling should strongly influence the final objective.

---

# Resource Extraction Philosophy

## Real Geology

The project should strongly prefer real materials.

Examples:

- Hematite
- Magnetite
- Chalcopyrite
- Galena
- Sphalerite
- Bauxite
- Cassiterite

Resources should exist because they make geological sense.

Learning industrial materials should become part of the experience.

---

# Mining Evolution

## Early Game

Traditional Factorio.

Visible surface deposits.

Simple mining drills.

Fast onboarding.

---

## Mid Game

Prospecting.

Geological surveys.

Resource exploration.

New deposits discovered rather than immediately visible.

---

## Late Game

Industrial mining complexes.

Ore-body extraction.

Instead of mining:

- Iron Ore
- Copper Ore

the player extracts:

- mixed mineral streams
- geological formations
- ore concentrates

Refining becomes a necessity rather than a contrived recipe chain.
